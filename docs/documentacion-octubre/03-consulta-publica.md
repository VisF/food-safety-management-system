# Consulta pública e inspección

## Índice

- Sección en español
- English section
## 1. Propósito

La consulta pública permite comprobar si existe un carnet asociado a un
CUIL/CUIT y consultar su vigencia. Está pensada para inspecciones y para
verificación rápida sin acceder al resto del trámite.

La aplicación utiliza CUIL/CUIT como dato de búsqueda, no DNI. Ingrese los 11
dígitos, sin espacios ni guiones.

## 2. Consultar un carnet

1. Abra **Consulta pública**.
2. Escriba el CUIL/CUIT del titular.
3. Seleccione **Consultar carnet**.
4. Compare el nombre mostrado con la identidad de la persona inspeccionada.
5. Revise el estado, número, fecha de emisión y fecha de vencimiento.

Cuando existe un registro, la pantalla muestra **VIGENTE** o **VENCIDO**.
También permite descargar el carnet y la foto asociada cuando esos archivos
están disponibles.

## 3. Interpretar el resultado

| Resultado | Significado |
|---|---|
| VIGENTE | La fecha actual se encuentra dentro de la vigencia registrada |
| VENCIDO | La fecha de vencimiento registrada ya pasó |
| Carnet no encontrado | No existe un carnet asociado al CUIL/CUIT ingresado o el dato fue escrito incorrectamente |

La consulta no debe interpretarse sin validar la identidad del titular. Si el
resultado no coincide, revise los 11 dígitos y vuelva a consultar.

## 4. Si no se encuentra el carnet

1. Compruebe que el CUIL/CUIT tenga 11 dígitos.
2. Quite guiones, espacios y caracteres extra.
3. Confirme el dato con la documentación de identidad.
4. Si continúa sin resultado, derive la consulta al personal administrativo.

Un trámite aprobado no siempre implica que el carnet ya esté disponible: el
administrador debe cargar el documento emitido por DIPA.

## 5. Privacidad y uso responsable

La consulta expone únicamente la información necesaria para verificar el
carnet. No copie, publique ni distribuya el número, la foto o el archivo
descargado fuera del procedimiento de inspección autorizado.

Los inspectores deben cerrar sesión al finalizar y no guardar credenciales en
equipos compartidos.

## Purpose and search data

Public consultation is a limited verification tool for inspectors, authorized staff, and other permitted users. It allows a person to check whether the system contains a food-handler certificate associated with a holder and whether the certificate is currently valid. It does not provide access to the holder's complete administrative process, personal profile, internal documents, or private history.

The application searches by CUIL/CUIT. Enter exactly eleven digits, without spaces, hyphens, dots, or other punctuation. This is important because an apparently valid identifier with an extra character may produce a “not found” result even when a certificate exists.

## How to verify a certificate

1. Open **Public Consultation** from the application entry point.
2. Enter the holder's eleven-digit CUIL/CUIT.
3. Select **Consult certificate** and wait for the result to load.
4. Compare the displayed holder information with the identity document of the person being inspected.
5. Check the certificate number, issue date, expiration date, and current status.
6. Download the certificate or associated photograph only when the system enables those actions and the inspection procedure authorizes them.

The identity comparison is essential. A result returned by the search must not be treated as proof for a different person with a similar name. If the displayed information does not match, stop the verification and review the identifier.

## Understanding the result

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **VALID** | The current date falls within the certificate validity period recorded in the system. |
| **EXPIRED** | The recorded expiration date has already passed. |
| **Certificate not found** | No certificate is associated with the entered CUIL/CUIT, or the identifier was entered incorrectly. |

The status is calculated from the certificate dates stored by administration. An approved examination or an approved administrative process does not automatically mean that a certificate is already available. The certificate must first be officially issued by DIPA and then uploaded by authorized administrative staff.

## When no certificate is found

1. Confirm that the identifier contains exactly eleven digits.
2. Remove hyphens, spaces, dots, and accidental characters.
3. Compare the number with the person's identity documentation.
4. Submit the query again after correcting the value.
5. If the result is still empty, refer the case to the responsible administrative area.

Do not create a duplicate account or registration simply because a public query returns no result. Administration must determine whether the process is still pending, whether DIPA issued the certificate, and whether the document was correctly loaded into this application.

## Privacy and responsible use

Public consultation must expose only the information necessary for certificate verification. Do not copy, publish, or distribute the CUIL/CUIT, certificate number, photograph, or downloaded file outside the authorized inspection procedure. Never use the endpoint to build lists of people or to search identifiers unrelated to a legitimate verification.

Inspectors and staff should close the session after finishing, especially on shared computers, and must not save credentials in the browser. Downloaded files must be handled according to the organization's privacy and retention rules. Any inconsistency, suspected misuse, or unexpected result should be reported through the established administrative channel without sending passwords or recovery tokens.
